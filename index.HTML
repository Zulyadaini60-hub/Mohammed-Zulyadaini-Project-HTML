Text Formatting

Strong text - used for important content (renders as bold).

Bold text - also bold but less semantic

Emphasized text - used for stress emphasis (renders as italic).

Underlined text - use sparingly as it can confuse with links.

Highlighted text - use to mark or highlight content.

Small text - for fine printer side notes.

Inserted text -shows content that has been added.
